Privacy Policy

DevChat is a personal, non-commercial demo project. This policy explains what data it handles.

What data is collected

When you sign in with Google, DevChat receives your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, and uses that information only to identify you inside the chat (displaying your name and avatar next to your messages).

Where it's stored

Your name, email, and the chat messages you send are stored in a Firestore database used by this app. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Third parties

Sign-in is handled by Google. No other third-party services receive your data.
